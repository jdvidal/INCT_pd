Dr João de Deus Vidal Jr

Postdoctoral researcher | Biodiversity Informatics | Macroecology

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Leipzig University, Germany. My research interests are unveiling the underlying mechanisms shaping the geographic distribution of Tropical mountain plants. I have expertise in macroecology, taxonomy, population genetics, and biogeography. I am a member of the Alexander von Humboldt Stiftung alumni network, Deutsche Zentrum für integrative Biodiversitätsforschung (iDiv) Halle-Jena-Leipzig, and the International Biogeography Society. Currently editing a special issue of Frontiers in Ecology & Evolution on Impacts and Adaptations in Montane Ecosystems Facing Climate Change .
